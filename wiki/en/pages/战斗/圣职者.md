The Cleric is one of the five [[Professions|professions]], and masters the {{item|Cross}}, with a {{item|Rosary}} at the belt. A Cleric's blows mend the Cleric, a prayer mends the Cleric and the companions near, and the cross strikes the undead and the night's creatures harder. Choose it from {{person|Bram}} in [[Firstlight Village]] from level 5.

## Starting gear

The first time you become a Cleric, Bram gives you:
- an **Iron Cross**: power 6, range 1, no element.
- a **Wooden Rosary**: defence 1, worn in the off hand; if the off hand is free it goes straight on. With a cross in hand, your mending is 20% stronger, and one blow in four that lands mends you 2 (the off-hand power “The Litany”; see {{item|Rosary}}).

The cross and the rosary are the Cleric's profession gear: only a Cleric can fight with a cross or wear a rosary, and the Cleric's talents that strike work only with a cross. A cross can be forged at an [[anvil|anvil]]; a rosary can be made once the Held Ground has its workshop; the Cleric's profession set is [[Gear Sets|Matins]].

## Mending

A Cleric mends in several ways:

- **One great draught**: {{skill|Prayer}}, the Paladin's {{skill|Healing Light}} and the Evangelist's {{skill|Psalm}} each mend a share of each one's own max health — you, and the companions following you within a few cells (the faithful and the angel you call, people who travel with you). With nobody hurt they cannot be cast. The shared {{skill|Mend}} counts as mending too, but mends only you.
- **Mending stronger**: {{skill|Oath of Light}}, {{skill|Blessed Hands}} and {{skill|Gospel}} make these mend a tenth to three tenths more, and they add up; with the rosary worn and a cross in hand, 20% more again.
- **Mending over time**: while {{skill|Consecrate}}, {{skill|Renew}} or {{skill|Divine Intervention}} lasts, you and your companions within 6 cells mend each second. These do not take the bonuses above.
- **Mended by striking**: {{skill|Faith}} and {{skill|Sainthood}} draw back health with each blow of the cross; the rosary's Litany now and then mends 2 more.

## How to play

- **Close in**: the cross reaches 1 cell, as a mace does. Faith and Sainthood mend you with every blow, and Steadfast, Iron Faith and Holy Vessel take the edge off what you take, so you can stand at the front.
- **At night and against the dead**: Blessed Arms strikes the undead and the night's creatures harder, [[圣域|holy ground]] burns the undead that walk onto it, and Vigil helps after dark.
- **The Paladin** fights with companions: Healing Light, Renew and Divine Intervention look after everyone, and Guardian Light draws the foes onto you. The third, the Evangelist, calls the faithful and an angel of its own, and mends them too.
- **The Avenger** grows fiercer the more it is struck: Vengeance strikes hardest at the one who just struck you, An Eye for an Eye strikes back hand to hand, Scourge makes the foe bleed and Torment bursts the bleed. The third, the Sentence of Suffering, lays the Five Sufferings on one foe blow by blow; the fifth, the lance, is a sure critical.
- **The Inquisitor** fixes on one foe: brand it with Brand the Heretic, hold it with Shackles or Confess, burn it with Burn the Heretic and Purging Flame, and finish with Sentence. The third, Doomsday, pronounces a Doom that falls five seconds later, and its Day of Wrath slays a branded heretic near its end outright.

## Boss Challenge

In the [[Boss Challenge|Boss Challenge]], the Cleric is level 20: health 144, attack 6, defence 2, holding an Iron Cross with a Wooden Rosary worn, with all twenty-two Cleric skills and the four shared skills Light Footed, Quick Hands, Strength and Vitality learned.
