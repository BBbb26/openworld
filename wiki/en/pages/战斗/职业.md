Your profession decides what weapons you fight with. You start with none. At level 5, go to the drill yard of [[Firstlight Village]] and find the profession trainer {{person|Bram}}, and choose one of the five professions: [[Assassin]], [[Ranger]], [[Mage]], [[Swordsman]], [[Cleric]]. Once you choose, the profession branch of the skill tree ({{key|K}}) grows that profession's six skills, and Bram hands you a starting weapon and the profession's off-hand piece. If you have not chosen by level 5, [[Lumi|Lumi]] begins the guide quest [[A Road of Your Own]] of her own accord and leads you to Bram.

## The five professions

{{profession table}}

- [[Assassin]]: masters the {{item|Dagger}}, with a {{item|Parrying Dagger}} in the off hand; strikes first from the dark and from behind, and finishes with chained blows. Starting gear: an iron dagger and an iron parrying dagger.
- [[Ranger]]: masters the {{item|Bow}}, with a {{item|Quiver}} in the off hand; standing firm, shoots true and far, and moves on once the shot is loosed. Starting gear: a wooden bow and a leather quiver.
- [[Mage]]: masters the {{item|Staff}}, with a {{item|Tome}} in the off hand; elemental spells echo, a mana shield guards the body, and felling a foe restores health. Starting gear: a wooden staff and a leather tome.
- [[Swordsman]]: masters the {{item|Katana}}, with a {{item|Scabbard}} at the hip; a quick draw, a true cut, one cut close on another. Starting gear: an iron katana and a wooden scabbard.
- [[Cleric]]: masters the {{item|Cross}}, with a {{item|Rosary}} in the off hand; swung as a mace is, its blows mend the one who swings it, and its light burns the night's things. Starting gear: an iron cross and a wooden rosary.

## How to choose

1. Reach **level 5** (see [[Levels and Experience]]).
2. Go to the drill yard in the northeast of [[Firstlight Village]] and find {{person|Bram}}: he stands on the drill yard day and night, with the sword-shaped mark of a profession trainer over his head.
3. Walk up to him and press {{key|E}} to talk; the “Choose a profession” panel opens. When you click to choose, you must be within 3 cells of him.
4. The panel shows five cards, each with the profession's description, its starting weapon and “10 skills”. Click “Choose”.

You can open the panel to look before level 5 too: at the top it says “Come back at level 5 (you are level N). Until then, look over the roads.”, and the “Choose” button is greyed out. In the skill tree, with no profession, hovering over the profession branch shows the tip: “No profession yet. At level 5, Bram the trainer sets you on one in the drill yard of Firstlight Village (crossed blades over his head): assassin, ranger, mage, swordsman or cleric.”

Once you have chosen:
- The profession branch of the skill tree becomes that profession's, and you can spend skill points on its twenty-six skills: twelve talents, five skill upgrades and nine active skills (see [[Skills#Profession|Skills]]).
- The **first time** you choose a profession, Bram hands you a starting weapon and the profession's off-hand piece (if the off hand is free, it goes straight on). Leave a profession and come back to it, and nothing more is handed out. An older save that chose a profession before the off-hand pieces existed is handed one as it loads.

## Second and third advancement

After the profession there are two more advancements (see [[转职|Advancement]]): at level 10 the **second advancement**, choosing one of the profession's three paths; at level 16 the **third advancement**, opening the profession's own advanced skills (the third follows the path, each with skills of its own). Both are done at a profession trainer ({{person|Bram}}, or {{person|Sergeant Kell}} at [[The Camp of the Red Host]]), and each gives 3 more skill points.

| Profession | Paths of the second advancement | Third |
|---|---|---|
| [[Assassin]] | Necromancer, Shadowdancer, Demon Hunter | By path: Hell Sovereign, Phantom, Ghostwail |
| [[Ranger]] | Hunter, Sniper, Skirmisher | By path: Greenwood Dawn, Hawkeye, Skywing |
| [[Mage]] | Cryomancer, Pyromancer, Stormcaller | By path: Everwinter, Worldfire, Wrath of Heaven |
| [[Swordsman]] | Sword Saint, Sacrificer, Spellblade | By path: Sword Heart, Mad Martyr, Nightbreaker |
| [[Cleric]] | Paladin, Avenger, Inquisitor | By path: Evangelist, Sentence of Suffering, Doomsday |

## Profession weapons

Daggers, bows, staves, katanas and crosses are profession weapons, each belonging to one profession; so is each profession's off-hand piece (the parrying dagger, the quiver, the tome, the scabbard, the rosary):

- **Only someone of that profession can fight with it.** Anyone else who attacks with it is told “Only a(n) X can fight with Y.”, and the blow does not land. Another profession's off-hand piece cannot be put on either (“Only a(n) X can wear Y.”).
- **Profession talents work only with the profession's own weapons.** The Assassin's talents count only daggers, the Ranger's only bows, the Mage's only staves, the Swordsman's only katanas, the Cleric's only crosses.
- Anyone can hold one, carry it, give it away or put it in a chest, and anyone can make a Wooden Bow at a workbench (see [[Crafting]]).

The other weapons are **common weapons**: swords, sabres, axes, clubs, maces, warhammers, spears, halberds and wands. Anyone can use them, but they take no profession talent, only the skills of the shared branches: Swiftness, Survival, Damage. The wand is a common weapon; the staff is the Mage's. The mace is a common weapon; the cross is the Cleric's.

Before you choose a profession, you can fight only with common weapons and tools.

## Changing profession

You can go back to a profession trainer at any time and change profession:

- On the panel your own profession is marked “Your profession”, and the others say “Change to this”.
- Before the change you are asked once: the skill points spent in the profession branch come back, and the old profession's skills are reset.
- After the change: every skill of the old profession (its path's and its third's with them) is forgotten and every point spent on them comes back; the path is cleared, to be chosen again in the new profession (the advancement stays, with no need to level again); the old profession's gear you wear is taken off (it stays in your bag). What you learned in Swiftness, Survival, Damage, Gathering and the other branches stays.
- Apart from changing profession, there is no other way to respec.

## Legendary Arms

The Assassin, the Ranger and the Mage each have two Legendary weapons; the Swordsman and the Cleric have none yet (see [[Legendary Arms]]). When a Seal-bearer falls, if whoever felled them has a profession, they drop the first of that profession's two that has not yet appeared in the world. Without a profession, nothing drops. See [[Bosses]].

## Tips

- Before level 5, the best weapon is a Stone Axe you make yourself (power 5); see [[Combat]].
- Try each profession first in the [[Boss Challenge|Boss Challenge]]: there every profession is level 20 with all six profession skills learned.
- Profession talents count only their own weapons: once you choose Ranger, stop fighting with an axe.
