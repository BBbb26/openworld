The **angel** is a [[召唤兽|called creature]] of the Evangelist ([[转职#三转：福音传道者（圣骑士）|the Paladin's third]]), sent down one at a time by the ultimate {{skill|Gate of Heaven}}. As the gate opens, every foe within 3 cells of the caller is struck first. Drawn as a spirit glowing gold.

## Numbers

- Sent down for a level-1 caller: health 50, attack 7, defence 2, sight 7 cells. A sixth more for each level the caller has above the first; {{skill|Apostles}} adds half again to its health and three tenths to its attack.
- It stays 30 seconds, then goes in a puff of smoke.

## Behaviour

It keeps by the caller's side and goes in hand to hand at any wild thing it sees. Like [[信徒|the faithful]], it shares what the caller lends companions, and the caller's mending mends it. See [[召唤兽|Called creatures]].
