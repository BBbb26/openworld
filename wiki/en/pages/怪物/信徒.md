**One of the faithful** is a [[召唤兽|called creature]] of the Evangelist ([[转职#三转：福音传道者（圣骑士）|the Paladin's third]]): {{skill|Call the Faithful}} brings two at a time. Drawn as a monk in a habit.

## Numbers

- Called by a level-1 caller: health 26, attack 4, sight 7 cells. A sixth more for each level the caller has above the first; {{skill|Apostles}} adds half again to its health and three tenths to its attack.
- It stays 30 seconds, then goes in a puff of smoke.

## Behaviour

It keeps by the caller's side and goes in hand to hand at any wild thing it sees. It is the caller's companion: it shares what the caller lends companions ({{skill|Gospel}}, {{skill|Blessing}}, {{skill|Hymn}}), and the caller's {{skill|Prayer}}, {{skill|Healing Light}} and {{skill|Psalm}} mend it too. With the caller's {{skill|Crown of Glory}} learned, every foe it fells mends the caller and the companions near by 3. See [[召唤兽|Called creatures]].
