The **Sword** is a common blade: anyone can fight with it, and no profession's talents count it. Its attack varies with [[Substances|substance]] and [[Quality|quality]], and it may carry an [[Elements|element]].

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Smithing**: [[Smithing|forge]] it at an [[anvil|anvil]] carrying a {{item|Smith Hammer}}: 2 Iron Ingots or Steel Bars plus 1 plank (recipe level 2).
- **Buying**: [[Garrick]] and [[Bram]] sell swords (see [[Trading|Trading]]).
- No swords are placed on the maps.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Fighting**: hold it and press {{key|Space}} to slash in front of you, or left-click a monster within 1 cell. A sword is a blade, and its blow counts as a “cut”. For the combat rules see [[Combat|Combat]].
- **Anyone can use it**: no profession's talents count it.
- **Cutting things**: left-clicking a tree, a chest or another thing that is not a person with a sword also strikes a blow. The damage follows the efficiency of a “cut” against the substance: on wood only half an axe's, on stone almost useless (see [[Durability and Breaking#Efficiency table|the efficiency table]]).
- With no weapon drawn ({{key|X}}), a left click on a friendly person first asks “Attack so-and-so?”; right-clicking a person with a sword uses the sword on them (asked first too), which the rules do not define, and [[The World Learns|the world learns]] answers.
