The **Rosary** is a string of beads the [[Cleric|Cleric]] wears in the off hand: told over in prayer, it makes the prayer go further. Only a Cleric can wear it. Defence 1. Made of wood or bone.

## Off-hand power: The Litany

With a {{item|Cross}} in hand:
- Mending 20% stronger: {{skill|Prayer}}, {{skill|Healing Light}}, {{skill|Psalm}}, {{skill|Mend}} and the other skills that mend in one great draught all mend a fifth more, counted together with the [[转职#圣骑士|Paladin]]'s {{skill|Oath of Light}} and {{skill|Blessed Hands}}.
- Each blow of the cross that lands has a one-in-four chance to mend you 2.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Choosing a profession**: the first time you choose [[Cleric|Cleric]] with [[Bram]], you receive a **Wooden Rosary** with an iron [[十字架|cross]]; if the off hand is free, it goes straight on.
- **Making it**: once the [[誓守之地|Held Ground]] has its workshop, string a wooden rosary from 2 {{item|Stick}} in the [[Crafting|crafting]] panel anywhere (anyone can make one; only a Cleric can wear it).
- **The set**: a **Matins Rosary** can be forged at an anvil (1 {{item|Plank}}, 1 {{item|Gold Ingot}}, recipe level 3); see [[Gear Sets|Gear Sets]].

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Worn in the off hand**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself, or equip it in the off-hand slot from the [[背包|bag]].
- **Only a Cleric can use it**: worn by another profession, or by someone who has not chosen one, it does nothing. See [[Equipment#Profession-only|Profession-only]].
- **In no set**: the main hand and the off hand count towards no set; the rosary has only its own numbers (see [[Gear Sets|Gear Sets]]).
- It is not a weapon: attacks use the weapon in the main hand.
