The **Cross** is the [[Cleric|Cleric]]'s profession weapon: a cross of the Church's, long in the shaft and heavy in the head, swung as a mace is. Only someone who has chosen the Cleric profession can fight with it, and the Cleric's talents that strike work only with a cross. Its attack depends on its [[Materials|material]] and [[Quality|quality]], and it may carry an [[Elements|element]].

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Choosing a profession**: the first time you choose [[Cleric|Cleric]] with [[Bram]], you receive an **Iron Cross** (attack 6), together with a wooden [[念珠|rosary]] worn in the off hand. Each profession's starting gear is given only once.
- **Smithing**: carrying a {{item|Smith Hammer}}, press {{key|E}} at an [[anvil|anvil]]: 2 ingots and 1 {{item|Plank}} make one, recipe level 1; iron ingots make an iron one, steel bars a steel one.
- **The set**: the Cleric's set includes a “Matins Cross”, forged from 2 {{item|Steel Bar}} and 1 {{item|Plank}} (recipe level 3; see [[Gear Sets|Gear Sets]]).
- No crosses are placed on the maps.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Fighting**: hold it and press {{key|Space}} to strike in front of you, or left-click a monster within 1 cell. A cross is a blunt weapon. For the combat rules see [[Combat|Combat]].
- **Only a Cleric can use it**: held by another profession, or by someone who has not chosen one, it makes no attack. It can still be held, put in a chest or given away.
- **The Cleric's talents**: {{skill|Faith}}, {{skill|Blessed Arms}}, {{skill|Zeal}} and the other talents that strike work only with a cross; the {{item|Rosary}} in the off hand works only with a cross in hand, too. See [[Cleric|Cleric]].
